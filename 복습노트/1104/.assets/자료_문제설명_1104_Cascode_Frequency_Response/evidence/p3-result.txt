Zout: (C_mu*s + C_pi*s + 1/r_pi + 1/R_B)/((C_mu*s + 1/R_B)*(C_pi*s + gm + 1/r_pi))
Pole corners: 1/(C_mu*R_B) (gm + 1/r_pi)/C_pi
Zero corner: (1/r_pi + 1/R_B)/(C_mu + C_pi)
Zout(0): (R_B + r_pi)/(gm*r_pi + 1) Zout(inf): 0
HF coefficient s*Zout: (C_mu*R_B*r_pi + C_pi*R_B*r_pi)/(C_mu*C_pi*R_B*r_pi)
Magnitude asymptotes start flat. Add 20 dB/dec at the zero and subtract 20 dB/dec at each pole in actual corner order; end at -20 dB/dec.
