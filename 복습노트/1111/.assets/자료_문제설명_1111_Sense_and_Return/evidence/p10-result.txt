A0 exact (ro1,ro2 finite) = -R_D*g_m1*g_m2*r_O1*r_O2/(R_D*g_m2*r_O2 + R_D + g_m2*r_O1*r_O2 + r_O1)

=== (b) CG M1 (input current at source) + follower M2 ===
