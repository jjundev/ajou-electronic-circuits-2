exact omega_u: omega0*sqrt(A0**2 - (A0*K + 1)**2)
omega_u/(A omega0) = sqrt(1-(K+1/A)^2); large A limit: sqrt(1 - K**2)
gain-bandwidth product: A0*omega0

Solve |H(jωu)|=1: ωu=A0ω0√[1−(K+1/A0)²], provided A0≥1+KA0. For KA0≫1 and K²≪1 this is approximately A0ω0, independent of K to leading order. The DC gain times −3 dB bandwidth equals A0ω0 exactly; the unity crossing is only approximately constant.
