A0 replica = beta*R_L/(r_pi+Rth)
effective A0 full KCL = (beta+K)*R_L/(r_pi+Rth)
Rin0 = (r_pi+Rth)/(beta+1)
Rout0 = R_L
Acl = A0/(1+K*A0)
Rin = Rin0*(1+K*A0)
Rout = Rout0/(1+K*A0)
Rth=R1||R2; R_L=RC||(R1+R2); K=R2/(R1+R2)
The finite beta exact forward term adds +K from the base-current path through R1.
All exact/replica ratios tend to 1 for beta -> infinity.
