A biased common-gate stage acts as a current buffer: its low input resistance and high output resistance decouple the two voltages. A changing output voltage has little effect on input voltage/current, and each side can operate at a different DC voltage within compliance. A wire ties the two voltages together. Unity current gain alone does not express this isolation.
