Under odd-mode differential excitation the common-source node is virtual ground. For a matched pair with large ro and negligible body effect, ωg=1/{Rs[Cgs1+(1+gm1/gm3)Cgd1]}, ωx=gm3/[Cdb1+Cgs3+Csb3+(1+gm3/gm1)Cgd1], ωo=1/[RD(Cgd3+Cdb3)]. Add any externally specified load to output capacitance. For identical cascode and input gm, each Miller factor is 2. The source–bulk term depends on body connection/model.
