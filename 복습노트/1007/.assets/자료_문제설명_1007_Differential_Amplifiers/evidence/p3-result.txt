a tail cascode resistance = gn*rt**2
a dominant Acm = -rp/(2*gn*rt**2)
b upper cascode resistance = gp*rp**2
b dominant Acm = -gp*rp**2/(2*rt)
General CM expression with effective upper load L, input ro rn, tail T: -gm rn L/(rn+L+2T(1+gm rn)); dominant result requires gm rn>>1 and 2Tgm rn>>rn+L.
full CM KCL gain = -L*g*r/(L + 2*T*g*r + 2*T + r)
b leading gain/scale = -a*b*c*d*e/(2*T*a*e + b*c*d)
parallel formula residual = 0
Therefore(b) requires Rupper || Rlower, not Rupper alone. Rupper=gm3 rO3 rO5, Rlower=2gm1 rO1 rO7; source reference agrees.
