Passive omega_p - omega_z = (C_1*R_1 - C_2*R_2)/(C_1*R_1*R_2*(C_1 + C_2))
Active omega_p - omega_z = (C_1*R_1 - C_2*R_2)/(C_1*C_2*R_1*R_2)
Both denominators are strictly positive, so coincidence is equivalent to R_1 C_1 = R_2 C_2.
At that equality both admittances share the factor 1+s R_1 C_1.
Passive: H(s) = R_2/(R_1 + R_2) = C_1/(C_1+C_2), constant.
Active: H(s) = -R_2/R_1 = -C_1/C_2, constant and inverting.
