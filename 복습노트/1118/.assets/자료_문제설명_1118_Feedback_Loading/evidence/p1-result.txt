b) Open-loop transimpedance = -a*b*g2 ohm
c) Textbook large-RF transimpedance = -a*b*g2/(a*b*g2/f + 1) ohm
Direct nodal finite-RF check = -b*(a*f*g1*g2 - 1)/(a*b*g1*g2 + b*g1 + f*g1 + 1) ohm. Dropped resistances b and 1/g1 are small vs f; input-output feedthrough negligible when f*g1*g2*a >> 1.
