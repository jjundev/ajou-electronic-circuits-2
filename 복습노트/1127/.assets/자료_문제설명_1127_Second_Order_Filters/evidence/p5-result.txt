D(omega) = omega**4 - 2*omega**2*omega_n**2 + omega_n**4 + omega**2*omega_n**2/Q**2
D'(omega) = 2 omega [2 omega^2 + omega_n^2(1/Q^2 - 2)].
With 0<Q<=1/sqrt(2), the bracket is strictly positive at every omega>0.
D>0 and d|H|^2/domega = -gamma^2 D'/D^2. For gamma!=0 the response decreases strictly on omega>0.
At Q=1/sqrt(2), D=omega_n^4+omega^4, so the flat derivative at DC still gives strict decay at positive frequencies.
DC is the one-sided global maximum. There is no interior positive-frequency peak.
If gamma=0, the response is identically zero and has no strict peak.
