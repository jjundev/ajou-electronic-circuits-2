Discriminant = -omega_n**2*(2*Q - 1)*(2*Q + 1)/Q**2
For positive Q and omega_n, the only zero of the discriminant is Q = 1/2
Denominator at coincidence = (omega_n + s)**2
Both pole locations are s = -omega_n; the common positive pole frequency is omega_n rad/s.
This describes the denominator poles, which persist as transfer poles absent numerator cancellation.
