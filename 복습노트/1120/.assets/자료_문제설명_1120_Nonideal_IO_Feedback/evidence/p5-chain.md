P5 E12.28. In the usual zero-channel-length-modulation model, j=gm1(vin-vs), vx=-RD1*j. Let S=R1+R2, P=R1||R2, K=R2/S, L=RD2||S. KCL at the divider tap gives vs=K*vout+P*j. The output balance with test current it entering the output is vout/L=(gm2*RD1+K)*j+it. The +K*j term is forward current from the M1 source through the divider, and the two inverting stages give a positive overall gain.

With the standard unilateral divider approximation, drop K*j against gm2*RD1*j (valid when gm2*RD1 >> K). The loaded open-loop voltage gain is A=gm1*RD1*gm2*L/(1+gm1*P), Rin=open circuit, Rout,open=L. Closed voltage gain A/(1+KA), closed Rin=infinite, closed Rout=L/(1+KA). For the output test set vin=0, so j=-gm1*K*vout/(1+gm1*P); substitute into the output balance to obtain the result. If that direct path is retained replace A by H=gm1*(gm2*RD1+K)*L/(1+gm1*P) in both closed formulas. No explicit assumption appears in the source-only question text; the unilateral result and the exact KCL result are distinguished. p5.py verifies both exact transfer and output impedance against three nodal equations.
