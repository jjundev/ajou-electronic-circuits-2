Fig. 12.45 wires K in parallel from the output node; this supplies node voltage, and does not define/decode the net output current.
For current-output, voltage-input feedback use a series current sensor in the load/test path, after the Rout split; retain Rout as a shunt to ground.
Proof with nominal output current pointing out: io=-Ix, VA=-K io, Ix=Vx/Rout-Gm VA. Therefore Vx/Ix=Rout(1+Gm K). K here has impedance units and is taken positive for negative feedback.
