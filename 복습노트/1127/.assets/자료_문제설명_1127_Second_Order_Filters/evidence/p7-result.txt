The ratio of asymptotic magnitudes |H(infinity)|/|H(0)| = omega_p/omega_z = omega_p/omega_z
For omega>0, the derivative of |H|^2 has the sign of omega_p-omega_z.
When omega_p=5 omega_z the high-frequency shelf is the passband, and its gain is 5 times the stopband gain.
A ratio of 10 requires omega_p/omega_z=10 for a high-frequency passband, or 1/10 for a low-frequency passband.
Therefore the proposed pole/zero ratio of 5 is impossible.
