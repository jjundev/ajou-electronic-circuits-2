Loaded replica: R0 = -h/(Yin*Yout) ; Rin0 = 1/Yin ; Rout0 = 1/Yout
Return convention: i_in+i_f, i_f=f*vo at vi=0; f>0; T=-f*R0.
Equivalent subtracting convention: i_in-K*vo with K=-f.
Loaded replica closed: R=R0/(1+T); Rin=Rin0/(1+T); Rout=Rout0/(1+T).
Exact closed physical circuit: replace h by h-f in the above expressions.
Exact D = Yin*Yout+f*(h-f); R=(f-h)/D; Rin=Yout/D; Rout=Yin/D.
Replica drops reciprocal forward feedthrough; R comparison requires |f/h| << 1.
Close to D=0, also check that the determinant change f**2 is negligible.
f=1/R_F; Yin=g_input+1/R_F; Yout=g_output+1/R_F.
g_input = 1/r_pi
g_output = (RC + r_o)/(RC*r_o)
h = gm
Open transresistance = -gm*(r_pi||RF)*(RC||ro||RF).
Open input = r_pi||RF; open output = RC||ro||RF.
Exact full circuit high-gm limit: negative transresistance -> -RF.
