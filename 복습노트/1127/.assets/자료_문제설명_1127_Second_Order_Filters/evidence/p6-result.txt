For omega>0, (|H|/|beta|)^(-2) = (omega_n^2/omega-omega)^2 + (omega_n/Q)^2.
The square is nonnegative and vanishes only at omega=omega_n, so the unique global peak is Q/omega_n.
The response vanishes at DC and tends to zero at infinity.
Plot coordinates are x=omega/omega_n and y=omega_n |H|/|beta|.
Divide plotted ordinates by omega_n to recover |H|/|beta|.
Q=2: sampled peak x=1, y=2
Q=4: sampled peak x=1, y=4
Q=8: sampled peak x=1, y=8
