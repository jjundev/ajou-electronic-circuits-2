With A(s)=−gmro/(1+sroCL) and the drawn subtraction, H=−gmro/[1−Kgmro+sroCL]. To obtain angular bandwidth B the signed block gain is K=(1−B ro CL)/(gmro). The negative-feedback gain magnitude is |K|=(B ro CL−1)/(gmro) for B≥1/(roCL), entering with an extra inversion. For B measured in Hz replace B throughout by 2πB. ro refers to the output resistance, with ideal current-source load.
