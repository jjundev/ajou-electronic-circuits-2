=== 1) 루프 절단: V_test -> M2 gate, V_ret = M1 drain(V_out), I_in = 0 ===
V_X/V_test   = -g_m2*r_o2

limit r_o1->oo: R_D1*g_m1*g_m2*r_o2

open-loop (g_m2=0): r_o2
